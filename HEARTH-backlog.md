## Open decisions on record
- **Merge tie-break asymmetry — left as-is, deliberately (25/07/2026, from the v351 bug review):** `mergeListItems` gives same-stamp ties to the REMOTE copy; `mergeNotes` and `mergeTripsData` give ties to LOCAL. The asymmetry is historical rather than designed, but it stays: once every mutation site stamps `updated` (fix-queue batch B) the tie-break almost never decides anything, and flipping either direction is a sync behaviour change with mixed-version risk for no user-visible gain. Do not "harmonise" it; do keep new merge helpers explicit about which way their tie goes.
- **Note star = a modification (25/07/2026):** starring/unstarring a note bumps `updatedAt`, so the editor's "Modified" date jumps on a star. Accepted trade-off — the alternative (a separate star stamp + a rule in `notesTs`) was rejected as machinery. Means the star syncs like any edit.
- **Backup restore semantics for bodyweight/BP (25/07/2026, fix-queue batch A):** additive union, keep-local on a same-date/same-ts collision — consistent with the rest of `importBackupData` being additive-only. Backup-wins rejected.
- **Growth date-edit collision (25/07/2026, fix-queue batch D):** refuse with a toast, never overwrite or keep-both — growth entries feed the WHO centile chart and the dosing weight, so silently discarding or duplicating a measurement is the worst outcome.
- **Week plan (v317):** household-shared chosen over device-local (it's coordination data, like grocery/recipes); dinner-only rolling next-7-days (no week navigation, no lunch/breakfast slots in v1); lives inside Recipes (no new nav icon, deliberate given pending nav consolidation); per-recipe "Add to grocery list" kept unchanged alongside it. Merge keyed on the day string (newest-wins) chosen over ids+tombstones so unassignment needs no deletion machinery. Drag-and-drop, nutrition APIs and recipe photos rejected (same fiddly-touch / third-party-dependency grounds as prior decisions).
- Add-to-grocery: **Option B** chosen (push items to Grocery **and** save a reusable chip in `fl4_recipes`). Saved chip stored at **base servings**. Re-tapping **updates the chip quietly + always re-adds the tagged items**.
- Add-to-grocery amounts (Phase 2): **Path B** chosen — amount is a **separate `item.amount` field**, not baked into the name (keeps name as dedupe key, avoids freeform parsing, and gives Phase 3 something to build on). Scale at **current stepper servings**; **new items get amounts, existing items re-tagged only** (never overwrite a hand-typed quantity). Base-vs-current divergence is deliberate: chip = base, grocery labels = current.
- Play Store + push notifications: rejected as disproportionate; app stays personal/family.
- Direct Claude↔GitHub editing: rejected to preserve the manual review checkpoint.
- Recipe sharing: started as **merge-by-id / incoming-wins** (v240), which clobbered edits when a stale device pushed. Replaced in **v242** with **newest-wins by `updated` timestamp + convergence re-push** on both the household and personal channels. Accepted costs: **deletions don't propagate**, and concurrent edits to the same recipe resolve as newest-edit-wins (older lost). Recipes are shared whenever a household is set (no per-section toggle).
- List sync model (v245): **union-by-id + per-item `updated` newest-wins + deletion tombstones**, applied identically on the household and personal channels (replaced the old household wholesale-replace, which lost concurrent adds). Tombstones (`fl4_tomb_<lt>`) are transient sync metadata, 90-day purge (7d→30d v289, →90d v295), deliberately excluded from backup/export.
- Recipe stores kept separate: `fl4_recipebook` (Recipes section) vs `fl4_recipes` (grocery quick-import).
- **"Today" view (v256): Option B** chosen — a manual **pick-for-today focus view**, not a recurring/daily-reset flag. **Persistent (no auto-reset)** so it adds zero new sync machinery (rides the per-item `updated` merge); surfaced as a **filter chip on General**, not a separate tab; **completing an item clears its `today` flag**; scoped to General only. The recurring/auto-clear alternative was rejected to avoid day-rollover + double-reset sync hazards.
- **Accidental-tap fix (v257): neutral-buffer approach** chosen — notes open only on the item body text, with a dead-zone gap beside the checkbox. **Long-press-to-open-notes was rejected** as too fiddly on touch (scroll-cancel, click-suppression, native context-menu collision, weak discoverability). Applies to all detail lists, not just General.
- **Weather location (v274): auto-detect with fallback** chosen — `navigator.geolocation` on every weather fetch, silently falling back to the hardcoded Carrigaline coords (v258) on denial/timeout/no support. **No manual Settings toggle** (rejected as one more thing to remember before/after travel). **Generic "Current location" label** chosen over an extra reverse-geocoding call to show the actual city name (rejected as an unnecessary third-party dependency for a "nice to have").
- **Trips section (v275):** named **"Trips"**, not "Travel" — the existing Lists→Travel packing list already owns that name. **Booking-level sync granularity** chosen over whole-trip newest-wins (a trip is a *container* of independently-editable bookings, so whole-trip replace risked clobbering a partner's edit to a different booking in the same trip — unlike a flat recipe). **Hideable nav entry** (added to `HIDEABLE_SECTIONS`, like Train/Notes/Home) rather than permanently pinned. Nav position: **end of the bottom bar** (confirmed via direct question, options were "right after Recipes" vs "at the very end"). Each leg of a multi-leg journey is its **own separate booking card** (no combined round-trip object). Deferred to a later version: countdown to departure, link to the Travel packing list, per-traveller passport info, budget.
